## Teaching tips {.collapsed}

Screencast mode
:   Command Palette, then **Developer: Toggle Screencast Mode**. Displays each
    key you press on screen, so students can follow your shortcuts.

Zoom
:   Scales the whole window for a projector. See
    [Navigation and layout](#navigation-and-layout).

Timeline
:   At the bottom of the Explorer, lists saved versions of the current file.
    Use it to recover an earlier version, even without Git.
